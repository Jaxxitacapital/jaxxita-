About meatballs

Built for Kabarak students, focused on meatballs only.

Start lean, serve consistent meatballs, collect meatball reviews, and grow into a complete meatball ordering platform when demand is proven.

  • Fresh meatball preparation and consistent meatball portions.
  • Free campus delivery for meatball orders around Kabarak.
  • Student meatball discounts and group meatball boxes.
  • A digital-first plan for meatball reviews, orders, updates, and loyalty.
